You are a Senior Product Designer, UX Researcher, Quantum Computing Domain Expert, UI/UX Engineer, and Full-Stack Software Architect.

Your task is to design and build a complete HUMAN-CENTERED web application for a:

"Teleportation-Based Quantum Digital Signature (QDS) Simulator & Threat Detection Platform"

IMPORTANT:
Do not blindly follow the requirements.
First understand the target users, their mental model, learning difficulties, workflow, and what they need to see.
Then design the product around user understanding.

The application must feel like a real professional software product, NOT like a college demo, generic dashboard, or AI-generated template.

==================================================
1. PRODUCT PURPOSE
==================================================

The software should allow a user to:

1. Generate Bell states.
2. Visualize entanglement.
3. Run quantum teleportation.
4. Understand how the quantum state moves from sender to receiver.
5. Show classical communication bits.
6. Apply Pauli corrections.
7. Verify the received signature state.
8. Perform projective measurements.
9. Measure states using X, Y and Z bases.
10. Calculate QBER.
11. Simulate decoy states where appropriate.
12. Detect possible tampering/noise statistically.
13. Simulate cyber/quantum attacks.
14. Compare normal communication vs attacked communication.
15. Understand WHY an attack is detected.

The application is both:
- an educational simulator
- a research/prototyping tool
- a security-analysis dashboard

32. IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC ACCURACY RULES
==================================================

Do not call every concept a theorem.

Correct terminology:

No-Cloning → theorem
Born Rule → rule/postulate-related formalism
Bell states → quantum states
Entanglement → quantum phenomenon
Teleportation → quantum protocol
Pauli X/Z → operators
MUB → measurement concept
QBER → metric
Decoy states → security technique
Intercept-resend → attack model
Phase shift → quantum operation/noise model
Decoherence → physical phenomenon

Never claim:

"High QBER proves an attacker."

Instead:

"High QBER indicates anomalous error/channel behavior and may indicate noise, implementation issues, or tampering."

Never claim:

"Teleportation sends matter."

Explain that quantum information/state is transferred using entanglement and classical communication.
